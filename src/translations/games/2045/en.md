In January 2020, the second ESIR Game Jam (my engineering school) was organized. This Game Jam's theme was "Safe In the Dark". With my group made of four people including another programmer, we decided to make a two-player local game where the first player is trying to escape from a prison. The second player helps him in achieving this goal after becoming "Shadow", an entity able to hide and deactivate every source of light. 2045 was made in 35 hours using Unity, Photoshop and Audacity.
