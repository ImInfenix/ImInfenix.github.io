Pop-Up run was my submission at the GDA Jam in 2019 made in a team of four people. The theme was "No Signal". I was the only programmer on the team as the three others were digital arts students. In Pop-Up Run, you have to find your way through the Internet with a runner-like gameplay while ads keep coming to cover your screen. You have to close enough ads to reach the end of the game where you can finally be at peace. But be careful! If you click on ads instead of closing them, more ads will be coming! The game was made using Unity, Photoshop, 3DS Max and Audacity.
