Stellaris: Grand Archive is a DLC for the science-fiction 4X game Stellaris (2016) developed by <a href="https://www.abrakam.com/" target="_blank" class="credit">Abrakam Entertainment</a> as a Work For Hire project in collaboration with <a href="https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/" target="_blank" class="credit">Paradox Interactive</a>, over which Abrakam had a high level of ownership. This means we were responsible for design and implementation from start to finish, and Paradox helped us during the whole development by providing their advice and help whenever needed. They also had the final word on what went into the DLC and what needed to be reworked.

This project was developed over the span of a year, starting from September 2023 and released in November 2024. It is the second DLC developed by Abrakam and reflects the lessons learned during the development of the first one, as it was greatly received by the community. The project runs using Paradox's internal engine, Clausewitz, which makes use of C++ to handle systems, and a custom script language to handle content for those.
